# Customization

Make MC Linker fit your server. Give it its own nickname, avatar, banner and bio in your Discord server, completely free. Or go further and run MC Linker as [your own bot](./custom-bot.md) (supporting us in the process!).

<img src="/assets/profilecustomization.png" alt="MC Linker customized as 'Villager Bot' with a villager avatar and banner" width="300" height="303" loading="lazy">

## Change how MC Linker looks in your server

<CommandInfo discord="Manage Server" where="Discord servers" needs="Nothing else, it works before you connect a Minecraft server" />

1. Run `/customize` in your server.
2. A form opens. Fill in what you want to change:

| Field | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Nickname** | The name MC Linker shows in your server (up to 32 characters). |
| **Bio** | The *About Me* text on MC Linker's profile in your server (up to 190 characters). |
| **Avatar** | The profile picture in your server. Upload an image. |
| **Banner** | The profile banner in your server. Upload an image. |

Leaving a field empty resets it to the default.

<BotReply command="/customize" title="Customized Server Appearance">Successfully customized MC Linker's appearance in this server!</BotReply>

These changes only apply to your server. Everywhere else MC Linker keeps its usual look.

::: tip Customization Failed?
The image may be invalid, or you changed things too quickly. Discord limits how often the bot's profile can be changed, and MC Linker tells you when you hit that limit ("Customizing Too Fast"). Use a PNG, JPG or GIF and try again later.
:::

## Want more?

With the **Customize MC Linker** subscription you can run MC Linker as your own bot: your own username, avatar, status and emoji, everywhere. Custom bots are temporarily unavailable, see [Custom bot](./custom-bot.md).
