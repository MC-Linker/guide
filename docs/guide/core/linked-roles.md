# Linked Roles

Discord Linked-Role connections grant a role upon linking and displaying a Minecraft badge on a member profile natively over OAuth2. It is an amazing way for your community to flex their in-game status directly in their Discord "Connections" tab.

### Setup

To set this up via OAuth2, instead of manually running `/account connect`, members will navigate to your server's settings menu or click on an existing user's Linked Role banner.

1. As a Discord Admin, go to **Server Settings** > **Roles** > **Linked Roles** and configure the role requirements. Look for the "MC Linker" connection.
2. In-game characters are authorized, meaning the link is fully secure.
3. Users click the Role to open their **Connections** settings, linking their Minecraft character.
4. They log into their server and use `/verify` when prompted safely.
